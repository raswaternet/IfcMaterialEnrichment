# Concept

IFC Material Enrichment helps teams answer practical questions like “How much concrete is in this model?” by combining:

- IFC model upload and inspection
- element-level material assignment
- bSDD-backed material lookup
- enriched IFC export
- material inventory reporting

Hackathon target:
- ship a clean end-to-end demo in 48 hours
- optimize for a strong 3-minute pitch
- keep the architecture simple and reliable
